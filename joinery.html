Joinery

Fitted & Structural Timber Work

Built in.
Built to last.

Joinery is the craft of making timber work with a building — fitted to walls, floors, and ceilings with the kind of precision that makes a space feel resolved. I commission and install every piece myself, from first measurement to final fit.

Whether it's a staircase that becomes the centrepiece of a hallway or a run of fitted wardrobes crafted to fill an awkward alcove, the approach is always the same: solid timber, traditional methods, no shortcuts.

All joinery is commissioned individually. I work with homeowners, architects, and interior designers across Nottingham and the surrounding counties.

How it works

From brief to installation

Every joinery commission follows a clear process — so you always know what's happening and when.

Consultation

We meet on-site or over the phone to talk through your brief. I'll take measurements, understand the space, and ask the right questions before anything else.

Design & Drawing

I'll produce detailed drawings and a clear specification — timber species, dimensions, jointing methods, and finish. You'll have a fixed quote before any work begins.

Workshop Build

Your joinery is made by hand in my workshop. Progress photos throughout, and final sign-off from you before anything leaves the bench.

Installation

I fit everything myself — precisely, cleanly, and with care for your home. The job isn't done until it looks like it was always there.
