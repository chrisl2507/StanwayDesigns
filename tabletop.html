Gaming Tables

Custom Gaming Tables

Purpose-built for Warhammer, D&D, and serious tabletop gaming

If you've ever tried to play a full campaign on a dining table with a mat on it, you know the problem. Dice rolling off the edge, drinks too close to the minis, nowhere to put your rulebooks. I build tables that solve all of that — and they look incredible in your home when you're not playing.

What you get

Built for serious gamers

Every gaming table I make is a proper piece of furniture first. Solid timber, traditional joinery, finished to the same standard as my dining tables.

Recessed Play Surface

The centrepiece of every table. Lift off the hardwood dining topper and you've got a sunken play area with a felt or neoprene base. Your minis stay put, dice stay contained, and the table edge acts as a natural barrier. When game night's over, the topper goes back on and it's a dining table again.

Player Accessories

Built-in dice trays at each player position. Cup holders set back from the play area — because nobody wants a spilled pint on their Ork army. Cable management channels for LED lighting rigs. Storage drawers for miniatures, rulebooks, dice, and character sheets.

Fully Customisable

You choose the size — four-player skirmish table or eight-seater epic campaign table. You choose the timber — Oak, Walnut, Ash, Cherry, or something else entirely. You choose the features. Want tablet holders for digital character sheets? Done. A specific play surface size for your Warhammer boards? Tell me the dimensions.

Removable Dining Topper

A hardwood dining topper that earns its place every day, not just game night. Crafted from the same timber as the table, it sits flush and looks like a proper dining table. Your gaming table doesn't need to be hidden away — it's a beautiful piece of furniture in its own right.

Pricing

£8,000 – £15,000

These are complex builds that take time to get right. The exact price depends on size, timber choice, and how many features you want built in. I'll give you a clear, detailed quote before any work starts — no surprises.

Tell me about your dream table

What games do you play? How many players? What features matter most? Send me the details and I'll put together a plan.
