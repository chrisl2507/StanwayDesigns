The Odyssey

From £2,500

The Odyssey is a statement storage and display piece, designed and handcrafted in our Nottingham workshop. Every unit is made to order, allowing full customisation of timber, dimensions, and configuration to suit your space and your needs.

Built using traditional joinery techniques, The Odyssey combines structural integrity with refined aesthetics. The result is a piece that feels substantial without being heavy — a balance of form and function that rewards closer inspection.

Design

The design draws on clean lines and considered proportions. Open shelving and enclosed storage work together to create a piece that displays what you want seen and conceals what you do not. Each compartment is hand-fitted, and the finish is applied by hand until it meets our standard.

Materials

The Odyssey can be crafted in any of our standard timbers — African Padauk, White Oak, Beech, English Yew, or Cherry. Custom timber requests are welcome. The choice of timber changes the character of the piece entirely, from the warm drama of Padauk to the quiet elegance of Beech.

Customisation

Every Odyssey is made to your specification. Height, width, depth, number of compartments, timber species, and finish are all yours to choose. We will work with you from initial consultation through to delivery, ensuring the finished piece fits your space exactly.

Interested in commissioning The Odyssey for your space? Get in touch to discuss your requirements and we will guide you through the options.
