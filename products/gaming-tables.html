Custom Gaming Tables

From £8,000

If you've ever tried to play Warhammer 40k or a full D&D campaign on a dining table with a mat on it, you know the pain. Dice rolling off the edge, drinks too close to the minis, nowhere to put your rulebooks. I build tables that solve all of that — and they look incredible in your home when you're not playing.

Every gaming table I make is a proper piece of furniture first. Solid timber, traditional joinery, finished to the same standard as my dining tables and desks. The difference is what's underneath the surface — literally.

Features Gamers Actually Care About

The centrepiece is the recessed play surface. Lift off the hardwood dining topper and you've got a sunken play area with a felt or neoprene base — your minis stay put, dice stay contained, and the table edge acts as a natural barrier. When game night's over, the topper goes back on and it's a dining table again.

Beyond the play surface, I build in everything you need: individual dice trays at each player position, cup holders set back from the play area (because nobody wants a spilled pint on their Ork army), cable management channels for LED lighting rigs, and storage drawers for rulebooks, dice, and character sheets.

Built to Your Spec

Every table is fully customisable. You choose the size — whether that's a four-player skirmish table or an eight-seater epic campaign table. You choose the timber — Oak, Walnut, Cherry, or something else entirely. And you choose the features. Want built-in tablet holders for digital character sheets? Done. Removable player stations? No problem. A specific play surface size for your Warhammer boards? Tell me the dimensions.

Pricing

Gaming tables start at £8,000 for a four-player table in standard timber, and go up to around £15,000 for larger tables with the full range of features. The exact price depends on size, timber choice, and how many extras you want built in. I'll give you a clear, detailed quote before any work starts — no surprises.

Tell me about your dream table. What games do you play? How many players? What features matter most to you? Send me the details and I'll put together a plan.
