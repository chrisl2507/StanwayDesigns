Handmade Wooden Film Props

Get in touch for a quote

This is one of the most fun things I do in the workshop. I hand-carve wooden replicas of swords, axes, shields, staffs, and hammers from real timber — not 3D printed, not resin cast, not foam. These are proper pieces of wood, shaped by hand, with real weight and real grain running through them.

Every piece is unique because the timber is unique. The grain pattern, the colour variation, the way light catches the surface — you can't replicate that with a printer. When you pick one up, it feels like something. It has substance.

What I Make

I work from reference images to carve replicas inspired by film, TV, and gaming. Viking-style battle axes, fantasy broadswords, elven-style blades, war hammers, wizard staffs, round shields — if it's a weapon or prop that can be carved from timber, I'm interested.

I use generic designs rather than direct copies of trademarked properties, but the results speak for themselves. Send me a reference image of what you're after and I'll tell you what's possible.

The Timber Makes It

I choose timber to match the character of each piece. Dark Walnut for something menacing. Pale Ash for something elvish. Rich Cherry for something with warmth. The wood does half the work — I just reveal what's already in there.

Perfect For

These pieces work brilliantly as display items mounted on a wall or shelf. They make unforgettable gifts for the fantasy or sci-fi fan in your life. And they're great for cosplay — lighter than metal, more authentic-looking than foam, and genuinely impressive up close.

Got a prop in mind? Send me a reference image and tell me what you're thinking. I'll let you know what timber would work best and give you a quote.
