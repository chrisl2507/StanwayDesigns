Centrepiece Bowls

Get in touch for pricing

These bowls start as a solid piece of timber and end up as something genuinely beautiful. The turning process reveals the grain in ways you can't predict until the wood is on the lathe — every bowl is shaped by the material as much as by my hands.

They're practical objects made beautiful by the timber itself. Fill them with fruit, use them as a key bowl by the door, or leave them empty on a table and let the wood do the talking. Either way, they earn their place.

Timber

I work with a range of hardwoods depending on what's available and what suits the bowl. Oak, Walnut, Cherry, Ash, and Yew all turn beautifully — each with its own character. Walnut gives you something dark and dramatic. Oak is honest and dependable. Yew, when I can get it, is extraordinary. If you have a preference, let me know and I'll source accordingly.

Size & Form

Every bowl is made to order, so the size and depth are yours to specify. Whether you want something small and refined for a bedside table or a large statement piece for a dining room, I'll turn it to your dimensions. The form follows the timber — I work with the natural shape of the piece rather than against it.

Finish

Finished with a food-safe oil that brings out the depth of the grain without obscuring it. The surface is smooth to the touch but not lacquered — it feels like wood, because it is. Over time, with use, it develops a natural patina that only improves the piece.

Tell me what timber you're drawn to, the size you're after, and what you'll use it for. I'll let you know what I can do and give you a price.
