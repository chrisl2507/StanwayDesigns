The Odyssey

Some pieces define what the workshop is capable of.

The Odyssey is a large-format bespoke dining table — a live-edge English walnut slab top with a hand-crafted base, finished to order. It exists as a reference point: this is the standard the workshop holds itself to.

Clients commission their own Odyssey-standard piece. No two are the same. The slab is selected together. The base is designed for the room. The finish is specified at commission stage and applied over days, not hours.

This is not a production piece with options. It is a commission in the fullest sense — designed from a conversation, built by hand, delivered as a singular object.

The commission

What an Odyssey-standard piece includes

Every element is discussed at consultation. Nothing is assumed.

  • 01 Live-Edge English Walnut Slab Bookmatched or single slab. The figure in the timber is part of the design.
  • 02 Hand-Crafted Base Designed specifically for each commission. Trestle, waterfall, or structural leg forms.
  • 03 Seats 8–12 Proportioned for a serious dining room. Smaller formats available on request.
  • 04 Hard Wax Oil Finish Enhances the natural figure. Repairable in situ. Will last generations.
  • 05 Full Commission Process Site visit, timber selection together, progress updates throughout the build.
How it works

From conversation to commission

The Odyssey process is more involved than a standard piece. That’s the point.

Consultation

A conversation about the room, the brief, the scale. A site visit is standard for commissions of this size.

Timber Sourcing

Slabs are sourced and you select the board. The figure, the edge, the character — all confirmed before work begins.

Workshop Build

Built by hand over several weeks. Progress photographs sent throughout. A deposit confirms the commission.

White Glove Delivery

Delivered and installed by Max personally. Positioned, levelled, finished. The job is not done until it’s right.
