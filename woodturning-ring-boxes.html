Custom
Ring Boxes

Turned by hand. Made to hold something that matters.

A box for the moment.

These are not jewellery boxes. They're precision-turned pieces designed to present a ring at the moment it's given — to be held, opened, and remembered. The timber, the weight, the way the lid sits: all of it is part of what that moment feels like.

Max turns each box individually on the lathe, selecting the blank for figure and character before a single cut is made. The interior is fitted by hand. No two boxes are the same — the grain, the colour, the natural variation in the timber is what gives each one its particular quality.

If you're commissioning a ring box, get in touch with a brief — timber preference, timeline, any engraving — and Max will come back to you directly.

Made to order

What you can specify

Every box is commissioned individually. These are the choices you make.

  • 01 Timber Choice English walnut, oak, cherry, yew, burr elm — or bring your own meaningful piece of wood. If you have a piece of timber that means something — a reclaimed beam, a fallen garden tree — Max can work with it.
  • 02 Interior Fitted velvet or suede lining, colour to order. The interior is cut and fitted by hand to hold the ring securely without movement.
  • 03 Personalisation Initials, a date, or a short phrase engraved on the base. Available on request — raise it when you get in touch and Max will confirm what's possible.
  • 04 Presentation Arrives wrapped and ready. No additional packaging needed — the box is the packaging. It can be handed over exactly as delivered.
How it works

Commission to delivery

Step 01

Commission

Get in touch with your brief — timber, lining colour, any engraving, and your timeline. Max will confirm availability and discuss the details.

Step 02

Turn & Finish

The blank is selected, turned on the lathe, fitted with the interior lining, and finished by hand. You'll receive a photo before it ships.

Step 03

Deliver

Shipped securely, wrapped and ready. Or collected from the workshop if you're local to Nottingham.

Most boxes are completed within 2–3 weeks of commission.
