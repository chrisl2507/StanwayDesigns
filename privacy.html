Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2026

Overview

Stanway Designs ("I", "my") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how I collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use my website or enquire about my services.

Information I Collect

When you submit an enquiry through my contact form, I collect:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Project details you provide
  • Timeline information (if provided)

How I Use Your Information

I use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to your enquiry about my services
  • Provide quotes and project proposals
  • Communicate with you about your commission
  • Maintain records of my business correspondence

Data Storage

Your contact form submissions are processed through Netlify Forms and stored securely. I retain enquiry information for up to 12 months unless you request earlier deletion.

Data Sharing

I do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties. Your information is used solely for the purposes stated above.

Cookies

This website does not use cookies or tracking scripts. I do not collect analytics data.

Your Rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Request a copy of the personal data I hold about you
  • Request correction of any incorrect data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Object to processing of your data

Contact

To exercise any of these rights, or if you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact me at:

maxstanway@stanwaydesigns.co.uk

Changes to This Policy

I may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
