How a commission works

Every commission follows the same careful process, from first conversation to final delivery

01

Consultation

02

Detailed sketches, material selection, and transparent pricing. 30% deposit to begin.

Your piece is crafted in our workshop over 4-12 weeks. Progress updates included.
