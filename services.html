How it works

From first chat to finished piece

Every commission follows the same careful process, from first conversation to final delivery

First, a conversation

We'll have a chat about what you're after — what the piece is for, the space it's going in, any ideas you have. In person or over the phone, whatever's easier.

Design and materials

I'll put together detailed sketches and help you choose the right timber. You'll get a clear price with no surprises. A 30% deposit gets things started.

I build it

Your piece is made by hand in my workshop over 4–12 weeks depending on complexity. I'll send you progress photos along the way.

Delivery and install

I deliver and install everything myself. I want to make sure it's right in your space. Balance is due before delivery.

Ready to begin?

Get in touch to discuss your project. I'll take it from there.
