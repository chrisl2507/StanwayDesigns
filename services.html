Our Services

Custom Commissions

Every piece is bespoke. Built to your exact needs, crafted to last generations.

Dining

Dining Tables & Chairs

A dining table is the centre of a home. It is where families gather, where conversations happen, where life unfolds. We build tables that earn their place in that role.

Every table is designed around how you live. The size of your room. The number of people you seat. The timber that speaks to you. We work with walnut, oak, ash, and reclaimed materials, finished to your preference.

Joinery is cut by hand. Surfaces are finished with care. The result is a piece built to be used daily and passed down through generations.

Living Room

Coffee Tables, TV Units & Consoles

Living room furniture has to work hard. It needs to be functional, beautiful, and built to withstand daily use. We create pieces that do all three without compromise.

Coffee tables with concealed storage. TV units designed to hide cables and clutter. Console tables that anchor a hallway or define a living space. Each piece is tailored to your room and how you use it.

We select timber for its grain and character, then finish it to complement your home. The result is furniture that feels intentional, not generic.

From £800 for coffee tables and consoles
From £1,200 for TV units and media storage

Bedroom

Bed Frames, Wardrobes & Storage

Bedroom furniture should be quiet, elegant, and built to last. We create pieces that bring calm to a space without demanding attention.

Bed frames with clean lines and honest joinery. Wardrobes designed to maximise storage while sitting naturally in the room. Dressers and side tables that serve their purpose without fuss.

How It Works

The Commission Process

Every commission follows the same careful process, from first conversation to final delivery.

01

Initial Consultation

We sit down — in person or by video — and talk through what you need. The room it will live in. How you will use it. What matters to you. This conversation shapes everything that follows.

02

Design & Quote

Based on our conversation, we develop a design. Sketches, measurements, timber selection. We provide a detailed quote with no hidden costs. If you are happy, we move forward with a 30% deposit to secure your place in the schedule.

03

Workshop

Your piece moves into the workshop. Timber is selected, joinery is cut, surfaces are finished by hand. Lead times are typically 4 to 12 weeks depending on complexity. We keep you updated as the work progresses.

04

Delivery & Installation

Once complete, the balance is due before delivery. We deliver and install your piece ourselves, ensuring it is positioned exactly as designed. Delivery is included across Nottingham and surrounding areas.

Materials

Timber & Finishes

We work primarily with walnut, oak, and ash, sourced locally wherever possible. We also work with reclaimed timber for clients who value the character and history that comes with it.

Walnut

Dark, rich, and warm. Walnut has a beautiful depth of colour and a tight, consistent grain. It ages gracefully and is our most requested timber for dining tables and statement pieces.

Oak

Strong, durable, and characterful. Oak has a pronounced grain and excellent structural properties. It works well for larger pieces and anything requiring robustness.

Ash

Light, elegant, and refined. Ash has a subtle grain and excellent workability. It suits contemporary designs and lighter spaces.

Reclaimed

Every piece tells a story. Reclaimed timber brings character, history, and unique markings that cannot be replicated. Perfect for clients who want something truly one-of-a-kind.

Finishes

We offer a range of finishes depending on the piece and how it will be used. Oils penetrate the timber and enhance its natural colour. Wax provides a soft, low-sheen finish ideal for pieces that will be handled often. Lacquer offers greater protection for high-use surfaces such as dining tables and worktops. We recommend the best finish for your specific commission during the design phase.
