Film Props

Wooden Film Prop Replicas

Hand-carved from real timber. Not 3D printed. Not resin.

This is one of the most fun things I do in my workshop. I hand-carve replica swords, axes, shields, staffs, and daggers from real timber — and the grain makes every single piece completely unique. When you pick one up, it has real weight and real substance. You can't get that from a printer.

What I make

Carved from real timber

I work from reference images to carve replicas inspired by film, TV, and gaming. Viking-style battle axes, fantasy broadswords, medieval longswords, elven-style blades, war hammers, wizard staffs, round shields.

Swords & Blades

Fantasy broadswords, medieval longswords, elven-style blades, and daggers. I choose the timber to match the character of each piece — dark Walnut for something menacing, pale Ash for something elvish, rich Cherry for warmth. The wood does half the work.

Axes & Hammers

Viking-style battle axes, war hammers, and polearms. These are some of the most satisfying pieces to carve — the weight and shape of the timber lends itself to something that feels genuinely impressive in your hands.

Shields & Staffs

Round shields, tower shields, wizard staffs, and walking staves. Larger pieces that make a real statement on a wall or in a display. The natural grain pattern gives each one a character that mass-produced replicas can't match.

Display, Gifts & Cosplay

These pieces work brilliantly mounted on a wall or shelf. They make unforgettable gifts for the fantasy or sci-fi fan in your life. And they're great for cosplay — lighter than metal, more authentic-looking than foam, and genuinely impressive up close.
