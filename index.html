Built the Right Way

We don't cut corners. Every piece is designed around how you'll use it, crafted from carefully selected timber, and finished by hand. Joinery is cut to traditional standards. Surfaces are treated properly. The result is furniture that improves with age.

Our workshop is based in Nottingham, where we work with walnut, oak, ash, and reclaimed materials. Each commission typically takes 4-12 weeks from consultation to delivery.

How It Works

Every commission follows the same careful process

01

Consultation
