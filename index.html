How I work

Traditional craft,
modern design

Every piece I make is built to last. I work with carefully selected timbers like African Padauk, White Oak, and Cherry, crafting commissioned pieces designed to become part of your home for generations.

My workshop in Nottingham is where raw timber becomes furniture — shaped by traditional joinery techniques and finished by hand until it's right.
