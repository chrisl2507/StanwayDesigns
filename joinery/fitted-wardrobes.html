Fitted
Wardrobes

Designed for the space. Built for keeps.

Every wall is a brief.

Fitted wardrobes designed and built to the exact dimensions of the room - not flat-pack units adapted to fit, not standard sizes with filler panels bolted on. The conversation starts with the space: the alcoves, the sloped ceilings, the walls that aren't quite square. What comes out is coherent with the room rather than placed in it.

Each wardrobe is designed from scratch, drawn to your measurements, and built in the workshop before being installed on-site. The internal configuration - rails, drawers, shelving, pull-out storage - is designed around what you actually own and how you use it, not a standard kit.

I carry out all the work, from the first site visit through to final fit. If something isn't right at installation, it gets fixed - no contractors, no middlemen.

The process

What’s included

Every fitted wardrobe commission covers the full scope - from measuring up to final finish.

  • 01 Site Survey & Design I measure the space and design around its character, not against it. Awkward angles, alcoves, and sloped ceilings are worked with, not around.
  • 02 Timber & Finish Selection Painted MDF, solid timber, veneered ply - whichever suits the space, the room's character, and your budget. Samples provided before any decision is made.
  • 03 Workshop Build Panels and components built in Nottingham to precise measurements. You'll receive a progress update before installation is scheduled.
  • 04 Interior Configuration Rails, drawers, shelving, pull-outs - designed around what you own, not a standard kit. Long-hang, double-hang, shoe storage, accessory drawers - whatever the brief requires.
  • 05 Installation & Finishing Full installation and final finishing on-site. Scribing to walls, fitting architraves and plinths, any on-site adjustments - until it looks like it was always there.

A note on alcoves

Alcoves are not a problem.
They’re a brief.

The space beside a chimney breast that previously collected clutter - that's where fitted joinery earns its place. A wardrobe or shelving unit built into an alcove doesn't just solve a storage problem; it makes the alcove a considered part of the room.

Alcove joinery is one of the more satisfying things to get right. The depth, the return walls, the way it meets the ceiling - when the tolerances are tight and the finish is clean, the result reads as architecture rather than furniture.
