Doors &
Frames

The detail that tells the whole story.

A solid timber door is a different thing entirely from a hollow-core panel. The weight of it, the sound of it closing, the way it holds a room. Max makes internal and external doors to commission — panel doors, ledge and brace, flush contemporary, or period-accurate reproductions for older properties.

Frames, linings, and architraves are part of the brief, not an afterthought. Every element is designed and built together so the finished opening reads as a considered piece rather than an assembly of parts.

What I make

Door & frame commissions

Every door is made to the exact opening. No standard sizes, no trimming to fit.

  • 01 Internal Doors Solid timber panel, flush, or glazed. Made to the exact opening, not trimmed to fit.
  • 02 External Doors Hardwood construction. Weatherproof joinery. Period or contemporary profiles.
  • 03 Door Frames & Linings Made to match the door. No standard sizes, no compromises on fit.
  • 04 Architraves & Surrounds Profiled or plain. Matched to existing period detail or designed fresh.
  • 05 Reproduction Work Period-accurate doors for listed buildings or sensitive restorations. Research-led.
  • 06 Garage & Outbuilding Doors Ledge and brace, or framed and braced. Solid hardwood or softwood.
How it works

Survey to installation

Each door is measured and built specifically for its opening.

Survey & Measurement

Max measures every opening on site. Existing frames assessed, any remedial work discussed upfront.

Design

Profile, timber, glazing, and ironmongery specified and agreed. Fixed price confirmed before build starts.

Workshop Build

Built by hand in Nottingham to exact measurements. Frames and doors dry-fitted before delivery.

Installation

Hung, fitted, and adjusted on site by Max. The door should close properly the first time — and every time after.
