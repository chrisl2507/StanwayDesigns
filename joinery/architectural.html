Architectural
Joinery

Rooms are defined by their edges.

Skirting boards, cornices, dado rails, window boards, panelling, pilasters — architectural joinery is the framework that gives a room its character. In period properties it needs to be accurate. In new builds it needs to be bold enough to compete with the architecture.

Max profiles, machines, and fits architectural timber elements to commission. Each job is measured and specified on site before anything is cut.

What I make

Architectural commissions

The details that make a room feel finished. Profiled and fitted to specification.

  • 01 Skirting Boards Solid timber, profiled to order. Period or contemporary. Mitred corners, no gaps.
  • 02 Cornicing & Coving Timber or composite to match. Run in situ or mitred from machined sections.
  • 03 Dado & Picture Rails Height and profile specified to the room.
  • 04 Wall Panelling Full-height, dado height, or feature wall. Shaker, Georgian, or contemporary flush.
  • 05 Window Boards & Reveals Deep sills and reveals that make the most of the window. Solid timber, properly finished.
  • 06 Pilasters & Columns Structural or decorative. Turned or fluted to the architectural brief.
How it works

Survey to finished room

Precision begins with the survey. The rest follows from there.

Survey & Detail Review

Max surveys the space, documents existing profiles, and discusses what needs to be matched or newly designed.

Machining & Workshop Prep

Profiles machined to specification in the workshop. Test sections produced and approved before full run.

Installation & Finishing

Fitted on site by Max. Scribing, mitring, and all fixings handled until every joint is tight and level.
