Built-In Shelving
& Cabinetry

Built in.
Not placed in.

Built-in shelving and cabinetry transforms a wall into a considered part of the room rather than a surface for things to accumulate against. Max designs and builds shelving and cabinetry for alcoves, chimney breasts, home offices, and utility spaces - open, closed, or mixed.

Always solid timber or quality ply, never flat-pack adapted. The difference is obvious the moment you see it.

What I build

Shelving & cabinetry commissions

Fitted to the room. Finished to last.

  • 01 Alcove Shelving The most-requested commission. Fitted exactly, painted or stained to finish.
  • 02 Floor-to-Ceiling Bookcases Proper library-weight construction. Adjustable shelves with solid uprights.
  • 03 Home Office Cabinetry Desks, drawers, and storage integrated into the room rather than added to it.
  • 04 Utility & Laundry Rooms Cabinetry that makes hard-working spaces function properly.
  • 05 Display & Media Cabinetry Shelving around a fireplace or media unit. Open, glazed, or solid doors.
How it works

Survey to finished installation

Every shelving commission starts with the space, not a catalogue.

Survey & Design

Max visits and measures the space. Design drawn with exact dimensions, shelf positions, and material finish agreed before build begins.

Workshop Build

Built in Nottingham to precise measurements. Components checked against the survey before delivery to site.

Installation & Paint/Finish

Fitted and finished on site by Max. Scribing to walls, final adjustments, and any on-site paint or stain work until it looks like it was always there.
