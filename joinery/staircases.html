Staircases &
Balustrades

The most structural piece of joinery in the house.

A staircase is seen every day. It defines the entrance of a home more than almost anything else. I build staircases in solid timber - oak, pine, or walnut - with balustrades and newel posts designed to match.

Straight flights, quarter-turn, half-turn, or open-riser floating designs. Each one is a commission from the structural drawings up.

What I build

Staircase commissions

Every staircase is engineered for the space, built for longevity, and finished to specification.

  • 01 Straight Flights Classic construction, properly done. Solid treads, no flex, no creak.
  • 02 Quarter & Half-Turn Staircases With winder treads or a landing. Designed for the space.
  • 03 Open Riser & Floating Designs Contemporary aesthetic. Engineered for safety and longevity.
  • 04 Balustrades Timber, steel, glass, or mixed. Newel posts turned or architectural to match.
  • 05 Handrails Profiled solid timber or steel. Continuous rail or post-to-post.
  • 06 Renovation & Replacement Existing staircase creaking or tired? Replacement treads, balusters, or full refit.
How it works

From brief to installation

Every staircase commission follows the same careful sequence.

Site Survey

I visit, take full measurements, and assess the structural context. No commission begins without this.

Design & Engineering

Drawings produced to your specification. Timber, balustrade style, and handrail profile agreed before build begins.

Workshop Build

Built by hand in Nottingham. Components dry-fitted in the workshop before delivery to site.

Installation

I install personally. On-site adjustments made until everything is exact.
