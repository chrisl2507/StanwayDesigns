About Stanway

The Craftsman

Every piece tells a story. This is ours.

Where It Started

Woodworking runs in the family. My father and uncle both began their careers specialising in office furniture design before moving into consulting. I grew up watching them sketch, problem-solve, and build. The language of timber and joinery was spoken at the dinner table.

I completed a BA in Furniture and Design, not because I needed permission to build, but because I wanted to understand the depth of the craft. The history. The techniques that have been refined over centuries. The discipline required to make something that lasts.

One of the first commissions I truly cared about was a ring box for my sister's wedding. Something small, deeply personal, and entirely hers. Walnut and brass, hand-fitted, with a lining chosen for the way it felt. That piece reminded me why this work matters — not scale or complexity, but the quiet permanence of something made with care.

The Workshop

Where The Work Happens

The workshop is purpose-built. Every tool has its place. The light is good. The space is designed for focus, not show.

This is where raw timber becomes furniture. Where sketches are tested, joints are fitted, and finishes are hand-applied until they are exactly right. The process is quiet, methodical, and unrushed.

Most clients never see this space, and that is fine. What matters is that the work done here speaks for itself when it arrives in your home.

Materials

The Timber We Use

We source timber locally wherever possible. The specifics matter less than the quality. Walnut for its depth and warmth. Oak for its strength and grain. Ash for its elegance and workability. Each piece is selected for character, not just consistency.

Sometimes clients bring their own materials — reclaimed beams from a family property, timber with a story already attached. We welcome that. The best commissions often start with a conversation about what the wood has already lived through.

What matters is that every piece of timber we work with is worthy of the time it takes to craft it properly.
