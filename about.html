About

The Craftsman

Where heritage meets modern craftsmanship

"The craft runs in the family"

My father and uncle both began their careers specialising in office furniture design before moving into consulting. I grew up watching them sketch, problem-solve, and build. The language of timber and joinery was spoken at the dinner table.

I completed a BA in Furniture and Design, not because I needed permission to build, but because I wanted to understand the depth of the craft. The history. The techniques that have been refined over centuries. The discipline required to make something that lasts.

One of the first commissions I truly cared about was a ring box for my sister's wedding. Something small, deeply personal, and entirely hers. Walnut and brass, hand-fitted, with a lining chosen for the way it felt. That piece reminded me why this work matters — not scale or complexity, but the quiet permanence of something made with care.

The Workshop

Where it happens

Purpose-built. Every tool has its place. The light is good. The space is designed for focus, not show. This is where raw timber becomes furniture. Where sketches are tested, joints are fitted, and finishes are hand-applied until they are exactly right.

Our Materials

The timber we choose

Each wood species brings its own character, grain pattern, and story. We select every piece for beauty and longevity.

African Padauk

Vivid reddish-orange tones that deepen to a rich burgundy over time. Exceptionally hard and durable, ideal for statement dining tables and decorative pieces that demand attention.

White Oak

Strong, water-resistant, and beautifully figured. A warm golden hue with pronounced grain that suits both contemporary and traditional designs. Our most versatile timber.

Beech

Pale, clean, and consistent. Fine grain with a smooth finish that takes stains beautifully. Ideal for kitchen cabinetry, children's furniture, and pieces requiring a refined, understated look.

English Yew

Rich amber and brown tones with dramatic swirling grain. One of Britain's most characterful native timbers. Each piece is unique — perfect for bespoke commissions and sculptural work.

Cherry

Warm reddish-brown tones that mature gracefully with exposure to light. Fine, straight grain with a natural lustre. Suited to elegant furniture, small goods, and bedroom pieces.

We source timber carefully, prioritising quality and character over convenience. Each piece is selected for its grain, colour, and structural integrity.

Sometimes clients bring their own materials — reclaimed beams from a family property, timber with a story already attached. We welcome that. The best commissions often start with a conversation about what the timber has already lived through.
