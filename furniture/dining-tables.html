Dining Tables

Built around the room.
Built around the life.

A dining table is the most used piece of furniture in most homes - and the most visible. The commission requires getting it right: the right scale for the room, the right timber for the light in it, the right form for the life lived around it.

Max works in solid timber throughout - walnut, oak, ash, cherry, or reclaimed boards selected with the brief in mind. Every table is quoted individually because every commission is different. The grain, the figure, the character of the slab are discussed at consultation, not assumed.

Live edge or traditional form. Fixed or extending. The brief determines everything. The result is a piece that functions perfectly and looks as if it could not have belonged anywhere else.

What’s included

Every dining table commission

Discussed and agreed before work begins. Nothing assumed, nothing added without sign-off.

  • 01 Solid Timber Construction No veneers. The top is the timber, all the way through.
  • 02 Timber Selection English walnut, oak, ash, cherry, or reclaimed. Max will advise on grain, figure, and character for the brief.
  • 03 Live Edge or Traditional Slab tops with natural edge retained, or clean-line traditional form. Both done properly.
  • 04 Extending Mechanisms Butterfly, draw-leaf, or drop-in leaf. Integrated without compromising the aesthetic.
  • 05 Base Design Trestle, pedestal, hairpin in steel, or solid timber legs. Designed as part of the piece, not added after.
  • 06 Finish Hard wax oil, Danish oil, or lacquer. Discussed at commission stage based on use and preference.
How it works

From brief to table

Every commission follows the same careful sequence.

Consultation

A conversation about the room, the brief, and the use. In person, by phone, or by email. No obligation.

Timber Selection

Boards are sourced and presented. You confirm the slab, the grain direction, and the form before any work begins.

Workshop Build

Your table is built by hand in Nottingham. Progress photographs throughout. Timeline agreed upfront.

Delivery & Installation

Delivered and installed by Max personally. The job is not done until the table is in position and right.
