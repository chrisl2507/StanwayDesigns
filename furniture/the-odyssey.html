A statement piece.

I designed the Odyssey as a statement storage and display piece. Every unit is made to order in my Nottingham workshop, so I can tailor the timber, dimensions, and layout to suit your space exactly.

Built using traditional joinery techniques, The Odyssey combines structural integrity with refined aesthetics. The result is a piece that feels substantial without being heavy — a balance of form and function that rewards closer inspection.

Open shelving and enclosed storage work together to create something that displays what you want seen and conceals what you do not. This is not flat-pack dressed up as furniture. It is the real thing.

What makes it

The design

Every element considered. Nothing arbitrary.

  • 01 Open & Enclosed Storage Shelves and enclosed compartments are designed together, not as an afterthought. Display what you choose; conceal what you do not.
  • 02 Hand-Fitted Compartments Each compartment is individually fitted to the unit. No mass-produced hardware. Every drawer and door closes the way good joinery should.
  • 03 Proportions to the Room Height, width, and depth are yours to specify. The Odyssey is sized to the space it will occupy — not the other way round.
  • 04 Traditional Joinery Mortice and tenon, hand-cut dovetails, solid timber throughout. No veneers, no MDF cores. The structure is the same material you see on the surface.
  • 05 Hand-Applied Finish The finish is applied by hand until it meets my standard — not the fastest method, but the right one. Hard wax oil, Danish oil, or lacquer depending on the brief.
