Gaming Tables

From £8,000 — made to order

A proper table.
For serious games.

If you've ever tried to play Warhammer 40k or a full D&D campaign on a dining table with a mat on it, you know the pain. Dice rolling off the edge, drinks too close to the minis, nowhere to put your rulebooks.

I build tables that solve all of that — and they look incredible in your home when you're not playing.

Every gaming table I make is a proper piece of furniture first. Solid timber, traditional joinery, finished to the same standard as my dining tables and desks. The difference is what's underneath the surface — literally.

From the workshop

Built as furniture first

What's built in

Features gamers actually care about

Everything considered. Nothing tacked on as an afterthought.

  • 01 Recessed Play Surface A sunken play area with felt or neoprene base. Your minis stay put, dice stay contained, and the table edge acts as a natural barrier.
  • 02 Hardwood Dining Topper Lift it off for game night; put it back on and it's a dining table again. No compromise on either use.
  • 03 Individual Dice Trays Built in at each player position. Not retrofitted — designed in from the start.
  • 04 Recessed Cup Holders Set back from the play area. Nobody wants a spilled pint on their Ork army.
  • 05 Cable Management Channels routed through the table for LED lighting rigs and charging. Clean, concealed, functional.
  • 06 Storage Drawers For rulebooks, dice, character sheets, and everything else that usually ends up in a box on the floor.

Built to your spec

Every table is fully customisable. You choose the size — whether that's a four-player skirmish table or an eight-seater epic campaign table.

You choose the timber — Oak, Walnut, Cherry, or something else. And you choose the features. Tablet holders for digital character sheets? Done. Removable player stations? No problem. A specific play surface size for your Warhammer boards? Tell me the dimensions.
