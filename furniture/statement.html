Statement Commissions

No catalogue.
No constraints.

Some commissions don’t fit a category. A sculptural hall piece. A hand-carved headboard. A live-edge bar top. A piece with embedded resin, steel, or stone. Max takes on a small number of statement commissions each year — pieces where the brief is unusual, the ambition is high, and the result needs to be singular.

Statement commissions require more dialogue. More iterations. More trust. The process is closer to a collaboration than a service. If you have an idea, bring it. The answer is probably yes.

What this involves

The statement commission

No standard brief. No standard outcome. Every element negotiated from scratch.

  • 01 Fully Bespoke Brief No starting point except the conversation. Sketches, references, or nothing at all.
  • 02 Mixed Materials Timber with steel, resin, stone, or glass. Structural and considered.
  • 03 Sculptural Forms Pieces that challenge what furniture is. Designed as objects as much as function.
  • 04 Collaborative Design Max works closely with clients on statement commissions. More iterations, more dialogue.
  • 05 Limited Availability A small number of statement commissions are taken each year. Enquire early.
How it works

Conversation to commission

More involved than a standard piece. That’s the nature of it.

Conversation

An open discussion about what you’re after. No brief is too unusual to consider.

Concept Development

Sketches, material samples, and scale models if needed. The concept is agreed before a price is fixed.

Build

Executed with the same care as any Stanway commission. Timeline and progress communicated throughout.

Delivery

Delivered and installed by Max. The piece is not handed over until it is exactly right.
