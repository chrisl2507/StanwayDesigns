How it works

From first conversation to finished piece

Every commission follows the same careful process, from brief to delivery.

Consultation

We talk through what you’re after - what the piece is for, the space it’s going in, any ideas you have. In person, over the phone, or by email.

Design & Drawing

I’ll put together detailed drawings and help you choose the right timber and finish. You’ll have a clear fixed price before anything starts. A deposit confirms the commission.

Workshop Build

Your piece is made by hand in my Nottingham workshop. I’ll send progress photos along the way. Timeline varies by complexity - I’ll be clear at the start.

Delivery & Installation

I deliver and install everything personally. The job isn’t done until the piece is in position and right. Balance is due before delivery.
