Bedroom Furniture

The room you return to.

Bedroom furniture commissioned by Stanway can be a complete set — bed frame, dressing table, wardrobes, bedside pieces — or a single statement piece designed to anchor the room. Either way, everything is built to the same standard in the same timber, so it reads as a collection rather than an accumulation.

The bedroom is where the brief is most personal. The dimensions, the storage, the finish, the drawer hardware — all of it is discussed and specified before work begins. Nothing assumed.

What I make

Bedroom commissions

Individual pieces or complete sets. Every item built to the same standard.

  • 01 Bed Frames Solid timber. Fixed headboard or upholstered panel. Storage base options available.
  • 02 Dressing Tables & Mirrors Designed with the room’s proportions in mind. Integrated or free-standing.
  • 03 Bedside Tables Matching or deliberately contrasting. Drawers, shelves, or open form.
  • 04 Blanket Boxes Storage at the foot of the bed. Hinged lid, solid construction.
  • 05 Complete Bedroom Sets All pieces designed together from the outset. One timber, one finish, one brief.
How it works

From brief to bedroom

Whether one piece or a full set, the process is the same.

Consultation

We talk through the room, the pieces you need, and how they should work together. In person where possible.

Design

Drawings produced, timber selected, finish specified. Fixed price confirmed before anything starts.

Workshop Build

Built by hand in Nottingham. Progress photographs throughout the build.

Delivery & Installation

Delivered and installed by Max. Everything positioned and checked before he leaves.
